# Vision

## What is Origami Vault?

Origami Vault is an open platform dedicated to preserving, sharing, discovering, and learning the art of origami.

Its mission is to become the world's most comprehensive digital library of origami knowledge, bringing together models, instructions, creators, techniques, and innovations from across the globe into a single accessible platform.

Origami Vault seeks to make origami more approachable for beginners, more rewarding for creators, and more accessible for future generations.

Beyond a library of models and instructions, Origami Vault aspires to become a digital foundation for the preservation, exchange, and evolution of origami knowledge.

---

## Why Does Origami Vault Exist?

Origami knowledge is currently scattered across books, websites, videos, forums, social media posts, and personal collections.

Many models, instructions, and original creations become difficult to find over time or disappear completely. Valuable knowledge is often lost when websites are abandoned, publications go out of print, or creators no longer maintain their content.

Origami Vault exists to preserve this knowledge and provide a permanent home where creators can share their work, receive proper attribution, collaborate with others, and inspire future generations.

The project aims to ensure that origami knowledge remains accessible, searchable, and organized for anyone interested in learning or contributing to the art.

---

## Who Is It For?

Origami Vault is for:

- Beginners learning their first folds
- Hobbyists looking for new models
- Teachers and educators
- Professional origami artists
- Original model designers
- Researchers and academics
- Software developers building origami-related tools
- Anyone interested in the art, science, engineering, or beauty of paper folding

Regardless of age, language, culture, location, or experience level, everyone should be able to discover and enjoy origami.

---

## Core Values

### Preservation

Origami knowledge should be preserved and remain accessible for future generations.

### Collaboration

The best ideas grow when shared. Origami Vault should encourage learning, sharing, and collaboration among creators and folders around the world.

### Respect

Creators deserve proper attribution, recognition, and control over how their work is shared.

### Accessibility

Origami should be easy to discover and learn regardless of skill level, background, or location.

### Openness

Whenever possible, Origami Vault should support open standards, interoperability, and community contributions.

### Simplicity

The platform should remain welcoming, intuitive, and easy to use, even as it grows in scale and capability.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Origami Vault aims to become the definitive destination for origami worldwide.

The platform will provide:

- A searchable library of origami models
- Step-by-step folding instructions
- Creator profiles and attribution
- Collections and favorites
- Model version history
- Support for digital origami formats and standards
- Interactive visualizations and folding experiences
- Community collaboration and knowledge sharing

Origami Vault should serve as both a learning platform and a permanent archive of origami knowledge.

---

## Beyond the Platform

Origami Vault aims not only to preserve origami knowledge, but also to support its future evolution.

As digital origami tools, educational systems, fabrication technologies, and research initiatives advance, Origami Vault aspires to serve as a foundation upon which new experiences and innovations can be built.

The project seeks to encourage interoperability, open standards, and structured representation of origami knowledge, allowing future applications, platforms, and technologies to access, share, and build upon this information.

The structured representation of origami knowledge may eventually enable the generation of visual, geometric, and machine-readable outputs from folding instructions and models. Such capabilities could support simulations, educational tools, 3D representations, fabrication workflows, research applications, and future technologies that extend beyond traditional paper folding.

While the initial focus is on creating a community-driven archive and learning platform, the long-term vision includes supporting:

- Digital origami representation
- Structured fold descriptions
- Interactive simulations
- Automated instruction generation
- Advanced visualization systems
- Educational and research platforms
- Third-party integrations and public APIs
- 3D model generation from origami data
- Future fabrication technologies
- Robotic and automated folding systems

Many of these technologies may not yet exist or may still be in their infancy. Origami Vault seeks to prepare for that future by preserving origami knowledge in forms that can be understood not only by people, but also by software and future machines.

---

## A Universal Language for Origami

One of the long-term aspirations of Origami Vault is to contribute to the development and adoption of structured standards for representing origami digitally.

Just as musical notation allows music to be shared, source code allows software to be reproduced, and file formats enable interoperability between applications, Origami Vault envisions a future where folding knowledge can be represented in a universal and machine-readable form.

Such representations could allow:

- Consistent exchange of origami models between applications
- Better preservation of folding knowledge
- Interactive educational experiences
- Simulation and visualization tools
- Automated fabrication systems
- Future technologies not yet imagined

While Origami Vault does not seek to redefine existing standards, it aims to support, expand, and help unify the digital representation of origami whenever possible.

---

## Guiding Principle

Technology is not the goal.

The goal is to preserve, share, celebrate, and advance the knowledge, creativity, and beauty of origami.

Every design decision, technical decision, and community decision should support this mission.

If a future generation learns a new model, discovers a creator, preserves a forgotten design, or invents a new way of folding paper because of Origami Vault, then the project has succeeded.
