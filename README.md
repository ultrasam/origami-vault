# Origami Vault

A universal platform for discovering, sharing, preserving, and learning origami.

Created by Sócrates Medina.

---

## The Dream

Origami Vault began with a simple idea:

What if there were a single place where the world's origami knowledge could be preserved, shared, explored, and expanded?

An open platform where beginners could learn, creators could share their work, educators could teach, researchers could collaborate, and future generations could discover the beauty of paper folding.

The idea for Origami Vault was born during a flight and has since evolved into a long-term vision of creating a digital home for origami knowledge and innovation.

---

## About

Origami Vault is an open project dedicated to preserving and advancing the art of origami.

Its mission is to become the world's most comprehensive digital library of origami models, folding instructions, creators, techniques, and knowledge.

The platform aims to provide:

- A searchable library of origami models
- Step-by-step folding instructions
- Creator profiles and attribution
- Community collaboration and sharing
- Support for digital origami standards and formats
- Long-term preservation of origami knowledge

Over time, Origami Vault aspires to become part of the digital infrastructure of origami, helping future tools, educational platforms, simulations, research projects, and technologies build upon a common foundation of structured origami knowledge.
