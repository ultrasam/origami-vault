# Origami Vault

A universal platform for discovering, sharing, preserving, and learning origami.

Created by Sócrates Medina.

---

## The Dream

Origami Vault began with a simple idea:

What if there were a single place where the world's origami knowledge could be preserved, shared, explored, and expanded?

An open platform where beginners could learn, creators could share their work, educators could teach, researchers could collaborate, and future generations could discover the beauty of paper folding.

The idea for Origami Vault was born during a flight and has since evolved into a long-term vision of creating a digital home for origami knowledge and innovation.

---

## About

Origami Vault is an open project dedicated to preserving and advancing the art of origami.

Its mission is to become the world's most comprehensive preservation and knowledge platform for:

- Origami works
- Origami editions and publications
- Folding instructions and educational content
- Creators and attribution records
- Techniques and taxonomy
- Notation systems
- Digital artifacts and preservation records
- Provenance and historical evidence
- Research and interoperability data

The platform aims to provide:

- A searchable library of origami works and models
- Step-by-step instructional content
- Creator profiles and attribution records
- Support for origami notation systems
- Support for FOLD and future interchange formats
- Provenance-aware preservation workflows
- Community collaboration and knowledge curation
- Long-term preservation of origami knowledge

Over time, Origami Vault aspires to become part of the digital infrastructure of origami, helping future tools, educational platforms, simulations, research projects, preservation initiatives, and interoperability standards build upon a common foundation of structured origami knowledge.

## What Makes Origami Vault Different?

Origami Vault is not simply a repository of folding instructions.

It is a preservation-first knowledge platform that distinguishes between works, editions, instructional content, attribution records, and digital artifacts while maintaining provenance for derived knowledge and supporting long-term interoperability across origami standards and formats.

---
## Core Knowledge Principles

Origami Vault is built around a canonical knowledge model that is independent of any specific technology, database, application, file format, or user interface.

Key principles include:

- Origami works, editions, and digital artifacts are distinct concepts.
- Knowledge is independent of any single interchange or storage format.
- Instructional knowledge and geometric representation are modeled separately.
- Original artifacts are preserved and never replaced.
- Provenance is retained for important knowledge and transformations.
- Attribution is a first-class concept.
- Unknown information is a valid and preservable state.
- Conflicting historical claims may coexist when evidence supports multiple interpretations.
- Knowledge should remain interoperable across present and future technologies.

These principles exist to ensure that origami knowledge can be preserved, understood, reused, and extended for generations to come.

---

## Architectural Foundation

Origami Vault maintains a canonical knowledge model that exists independently from any external representation.

Supported representations may include:

- FOLD
- PDF
- SVG
- PNG
- JPEG
- Video formats
- Future interchange formats

FOLD is considered a first-class preservation and interoperability artifact. However, FOLD is not the canonical representation of origami knowledge within Origami Vault. The platform preserves FOLD files, derives knowledge from them, and links them to the broader knowledge model alongside other artifact types.

---

## Core Knowledge Domains

The platform is organized around a set of conceptual domains:

- Catalog Domain
- Attribution Domain
- Rights Domain
- Instruction Domain
- Artifact Domain
- FOLD Domain
- Provenance Domain
- Taxonomy Domain
- Notation Domain
- External Reference Domain
- Community Domain

Each domain owns specific concepts while maintaining explicit relationships with other domains. Together they form the canonical knowledge model of Origami Vault.

## MVP Focus
The initial releases of Origami Vault will prioritize:

- Preservation
- Cataloging
- Attribution
- Instructional content
- Provenance
- Interoperability

Primary MVP domains:
- Catalog
- Attribution
- Rights
- Instruction
- Artifact
- FOLD
- Provenance
- External Reference
- Taxonomy (categories and tags)

Future releases may expand into community systems, reputation mechanisms, advanced notation support, geometry editing, simulation capabilities, and additional interchange standards.
